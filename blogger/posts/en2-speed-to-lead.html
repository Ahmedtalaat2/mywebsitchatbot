Most brokerages track how many leads they get. Very few track the number that decides whether those leads turn into deals: how fast they reply.

That number has a name: speed to lead. And for most real estate teams, it's the cheapest way to get more viewings without spending one more dirham on ads.

What is speed to lead?

Speed to lead is the time between a lead contacting you (a Meta lead form, a WhatsApp message, a website enquiry) and your first real reply. Not an auto-reply that says "we'll get back to you." A reply that moves the conversation forward.

What the research says

In "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", published in Harvard Business Review in 2011, researchers James Oldroyd, Kristina McElheran and David Elkington tested how 2,241 U.S. companies handled a new web lead. What they found:

  • 37% replied within an hour.
  • The average reply took 42 hours.
  • 23% never replied at all.
  • Companies that replied within an hour were nearly 7 times more likely to qualify the lead.

Think about that for a property buyer. In 42 hours, they've already spoken to several brokers, seen two units and maybe made an offer.

Why real estate is hit harder than most industries

  1. Buyers message several brokers at once. The same listing often appears with more than one agency.
  2. Leads arrive outside office hours. People scroll property ads at night and on weekends, exactly when teams are offline.
  3. Leads come in many languages. In Dubai, an agent who only speaks English or Arabic can't reply to everyone well.
  4. Agents are busy showing properties. The best agent is often in a viewing when a new lead arrives.

How to measure your speed to lead today

You don't need software to start. Do this test this week:

  1. Fill in one of your own Meta lead forms with a test number at 10 PM.
  2. Send a WhatsApp enquiry to your business number on Friday afternoon.
  3. Write down when the first real reply arrives, and what it says.

If the reply took more than an hour, you're losing leads you already paid for.

How to cut your response time to seconds

There are three ways teams try to fix this:

  • Hire more agents or a night shift. Expensive, and still slow on weekends.
  • Use a basic auto-reply. Fast, but it doesn't qualify anyone or book anything.

A simple speed-to-lead checklist

  • Every lead gets a real reply within 5 minutes, 24/7.
  • The first reply asks one useful question (budget, area or timeline).
  • Hot leads reach an agent the same hour.
  • Every viewing gets a reminder.
  • Quiet leads get a follow-up after 24 hours.
  • You can see all of it in one sheet or CRM.

If you can't tick all six, start with the first one. It has the biggest impact.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good speed to lead in real estate?

Under one hour is the minimum the research supports. With automation, replying within seconds is realistic, at any hour.

Does a fast reply really change results?

The HBR study found that companies replying within an hour were nearly 7 times more likely to qualify a lead. Replying first also means you're the broker the buyer talks to first.

Can I improve speed to lead without hiring?

Yes. A WhatsApp AI assistant can handle the first reply and qualification, so your agents only step in when the lead is ready.
