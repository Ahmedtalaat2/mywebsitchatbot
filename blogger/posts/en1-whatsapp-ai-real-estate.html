Why real estate leads go cold so fast

Buyers don't wait. When someone clicks a property ad, they're interested right now, and they usually message more than one broker at the same time.

Research backs this up. In a well-known Harvard Business Review study, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011), researchers audited 2,241 U.S. companies and found:

  • The average first response to a web lead took 42 hours.
  • 23% of companies never responded at all.
  • Companies that responded within an hour were nearly 7 times more likely to qualify the lead than those that waited longer.

The study wasn't about real estate, but anyone who has worked with Meta lead ads in Dubai knows the pattern: the first broker to answer gets the conversation.

Does AI replace real estate agents?

No. And it shouldn't. The chatbot handles the part agents are worst at: replying instantly, at any hour, to every single lead. Agents do what they're best at: building trust, showing the property and closing.

Your agents can see every conversation and take over the chat at any moment.

5 things to check before you choose a real estate chatbot

  1. Does it work on WhatsApp? In the UAE, buyers live on WhatsApp. A website-only chat widget misses most of them.
  2. Can it qualify, not just reply? "Thanks, an agent will contact you" is not enough.
  3. Does it book viewings? The goal is a booked viewing, not a long chat.
  4. Can your team take over? A human handoff must be one tap away.
  5. Does it connect to your ads? Meta lead form leads should get a WhatsApp message automatically.
