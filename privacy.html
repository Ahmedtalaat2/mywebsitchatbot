Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. This page explains what this website collects and why.

Who runs this website

This website belongs to Ahmed Al Gamal, a marketing and automation freelancer based in Dubai, UAE. For any privacy question, email ahmed.talaat11.6.2000@gmail.com or message me on WhatsApp.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, mobile number, email, the service you choose, your message, and which page or ad brought you to the site. I use this only to reply to you and follow up on your request.
  • AI assistant chat: the messages you type, so the assistant can answer and I can follow up if you ask me to.
  • Usage data: pages visited, clicks, device type and approximate location, collected by analytics tools.

Tools this site uses

  • Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful.
  • Meta Pixel to measure ads on Facebook and Instagram and show relevant ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity to see heatmaps and anonymous session recordings, so I can improve the site.
  • Google Sheets and n8n to store form requests and run the AI assistant.

These tools may use cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

What I don't do

I don't sell your data, and I don't share it with anyone except the tools listed above, which process it to make this site work.

How long I keep it

I keep contact requests for as long as needed to reply and work with you, then delete them on request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent me. Email or WhatsApp me and I'll handle it within 30 days.
